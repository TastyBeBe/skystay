**OWN-WORLD.** A two-colour letterpress reference book: warm bible-paper `#FAF8F4` with a second toned stock `#F2EEE6`, black ink `#16150F`, and exactly one second ink, deep green `#1F4B3F`. Two inks is a hard constraint, not a palette suggestion — there is no third colour anywhere, and every photograph is duotoned into paper-and-ink or paper-and-green so it reads as printed on the same press.
